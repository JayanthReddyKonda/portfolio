## 2. Deep Dive: Interactive Core Systems

### A. 3D WebGL Avatar & Studio Rig
* **Skeletal Parallax Tracking**: The 3D avatar tracks cursor and touch pointer coordinates in real time via dampened skeletal bone rotations (head, neck, spine).
* **Cinematic 3-Point Lighting**: An integrated key, fill, and emerald rim lighting setup provides sharp silhouette definition against the dark velvet background.
* **Proportional Multi-Viewport Framing**: The avatar is framed to maintain balanced vertical occupancy across desktop columns, tablet displays, and mobile viewports with zero clipping or awkward text overlaps.

### B. Neural ASCII Scan Matrix (`AsciiReveal`)
* **Dual-Layer Rendering**: Combines a high-density monospace character matrix with a masked raster portrait.
* **Proximity Scan Mechanics**: Cursor and touch movements reveal high-definition photographic details beneath the ASCII glyph field through an exponential falloff radius, evoking an AI biometric scan.

### C. 3D Bilinear Skinning Sticker Deck (`StickerPeel`)
* **Physical Mesh Deformation**: Interactive technology badges (PyTorch, FastAPI, Qdrant, Docker, PostgreSQL) physically peel and curve along a 3D parabolic surface when hovered or dragged.
* **Deferred Initialization**: WebGL context creation is deferred until the container enters the viewport, ensuring zero impact on initial navigation speed.

### D. Infinite Architecture Corridor (`GalleryTunnel`)
* **Procedural 3D Tunnel**: Renders architectural blueprints floating in an infinite perspective corridor.
* **Kinetic Navigation**: Scroll and pointer velocity modulate camera speed and depth parallax, allowing visitors to inspect system topologies in a continuous loop.

### E. Developer CLI Diagnostics Terminal (`TerminalWidget`)
* **Keyboard-First Navigation**: A fully functional command-line emulator supporting commands such as `help`, `skills`, `projects`, `metrics`, `clear`, `theme`, and `contact`.
* **Command History & Autocompletion**: Supports standard terminal conventions including up/down history navigation, TAB auto-completion, and formatted ANSI output blocks.

### F. Butterfly Drift Particle Field (`ButterflyDrift`)
* **Ambient Atmosphere**: A lightweight background particle simulation featuring floating emerald butterflies and drifting embers, providing subtle atmospheric life without competing with foreground content.
